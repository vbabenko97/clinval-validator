# Phase 4 - Storyboard

## Headline (single sentence)

**"The recovery is not done - it is done on Maui. Oahu is still losing visitors, the decline is accelerating, and 78% of the loss concentrates in 10 specific hotel segments we can target."**

This is the line the director should take away if they read nothing else.

## Audience

Director of tourism authority. Knows the business, doesn't look at data much. Needs plain-language interpretation, action-oriented chart titles, and a short ranked list of recommendations they can sign off on.

## Narrative coherence re-read

- Beat 1 acknowledges the director's claim without arguing. It's a setup, not a takedown.
- Beat 2 is the turn: it shows the aggregate is arithmetically correct but materially misleading.
- Beat 3 forecloses the "Oahu is just adjusting" rebuttal by showing trajectory.
- Beat 4-7 drill: hotel -> tier -> origin -> substitution -> concentration. Each beat earns its place because each narrows the action.
- Beat 8 is the ask. It must land on things the director can approve in a single meeting.

Every beat maps to at least one validated finding. Removing any beat breaks the argument (try: remove Beat 4, and Beat 5's "hotel-luxury" has no setup; remove Beat 7, and the recommendations feel vague).
